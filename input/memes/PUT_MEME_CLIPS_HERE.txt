PUT YOUR MEME CLIPS HERE
========================

This folder can contain MULTIPLE video files.

These are the clips where you're scrolling through funny memes.

Each meme clip will generate one complete video!
Example: 10 meme clips = 10 final videos

TIP: Name your files descriptively!
  - funny_cat.mp4
  - sports_fail.mp4
  - cooking_meme.mp4

The file name will appear in the output video name.

Supported formats: .mp4, .mov, .avi, .mkv, .m4v

Delete this text file after adding your videos.
